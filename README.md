## 🌟 Overview

**Nilkantha Polytechnic Institute (NPI)** is a leading government technical constituent institution established under CTEVT in close partnership with Nilkantha Municipality. Situated in Khanigaun, Dhading (near Dhadingbesi headquarters), it delivers practice-oriented diploma engineering education equipped with on-campus student residential hostel facilities and modern engineering workshops.

---

## 🚀 Key Programs & Features

1. **🏗️ 3-Year Diploma Programs (Total 96 Seats Annual Intake):**
   - **Diploma in Civil Engineering (Hydropower Specialization):** 48 Seats
   - **Diploma in Automobile Engineering:** 48 Seats
   - Short-term vocational skill development courses (CTEVT)

2. **🎓 Full Municipal & District-Wide Scholarship Quotas:**
   - **Nilkantha Municipality Ward Quotas:** Full scholarship for 2 students per ward (Wards 1 to 14 = 28 Seats).
   - **Dhading District Palika Quotas:** Full scholarship for 3 students from each other 8 municipalities/rural municipalities (24 Seats).
   - **CTEVT Classified Scholarships:** 10% central full merit scholarships (10 Seats).
   - **Open Merit:** Regular seats through entrance examination.

3. **🏢 Institutional Facilities:**
   - **On-Campus Student Hostel (छात्रबास):** Dedicated residential facilities for outstation students supported by Bagmati Province Government.
   - **Automobile Workshop:** Engine overhaul, chassis repair, vehicle electrical systems, and computerized diagnostics.
   - **Surveying & Geomatics Lab:** Electronic Total Stations, Digital Theodolites, Auto-levels, and field survey camps.
   - **Civil Testing Lab:** Concrete compression testing machines, soil mechanics, and construction material quality testing.
   - **AutoCAD & Computer Lab:** 2D/3D drafting, structural modeling, and estimating software.
   - **Central Library & Canteen:** Technical textbooks, digital resources, hygienic cafeteria, and open sports ground.

4. **📝 Interactive Portals:**
   - **SEE GPA Calculator:** Validates eligibility (Science & Math $\ge$ C, English $\ge$ D+, GPA $\ge$ 1.6).
   - **4-Step Online Admission Wizard:** Real-time application with instant printable CTEVT Admit Slip and QR verification.
   - **Admin Management Portal (`/admin`):** Default PIN `2083`.
